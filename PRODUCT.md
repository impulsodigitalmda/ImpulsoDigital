## Platform

web

## Users

Small businesses and independent professionals in Argentina. They get customer inquiries and orders through WhatsApp and lose track of them: messages pile up, orders end up in audio notes or loose spreadsheets, and the owner relies on memory. The studio started on the Costa Atlántica (Mar de Ajó) and examples come from coastal trades (rotiserías, inmobiliarias, hoteles), but the market is any small business or professional in the country. The coast is where it started, not a limit.

Their job when visiting the site: understand whether this person can fix their particular mess, roughly what it costs, and how to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Impulso Digital designs and builds connected digital systems for small businesses: a website, an organized WhatsApp channel, an order/stock/customer panel, and automations or an AI assistant. Each piece works alone; the value is in connecting them so an inquiry turns into a tracked order and the customer is notified at every step without manual work.

Success for the site: a qualified visitor reaches out (contact form → WhatsApp, or WhatsApp directly).

## Positioning

A single person, Diego Almaraz, handles the project from diagnosis through build and stays involved afterward. The client talks to him the whole time. Work starts with the one piece that hurts most and grows later, rather than as a large package up front.

## Product Principles

1. **Start with the piece that hurts most.** Lead with one concrete problem fixed, then the connected system as the next step.
2. **Speak in the owner's day.** Show real moments (a 22:40 order, an off-hours inquiry), not abstract features.
3. **A person, not an agency.** Direct, accountable, present after delivery.
4. **Honest proof only.** Examples stay labeled as examples until real client evidence is supplied.
